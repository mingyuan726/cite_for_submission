Before you go

Turn an event listing into a real plan.

  1. 1
    Confirm the details

    Open the Rutgers event page on the day you plan to attend and check for location or time updates.

  2. 2
    Register when required

    Some orientation sessions need registration or use a Rutgers Zoom link; the Involvement Fair does not require an attendee RSVP.

  3. 3
    Bring one question

    Ask about a club, campus service, or next step. A small conversation is often the easiest way to start building community.
