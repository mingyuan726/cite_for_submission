Community guidelines

Helpful, respectful, and safe.

  • Be respectful of different identities, experiences, and opinions.
  • Do not publish phone numbers, home addresses, passwords, or private records.
  • Verify academic, financial, health, and immigration guidance with an official Rutgers office.
  • For an immediate emergency, call 911 or Rutgers University Police at 732-932-7211.
