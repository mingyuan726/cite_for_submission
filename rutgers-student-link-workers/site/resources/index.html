How the Learning Centers support students

This video introduces tutoring, academic coaching, study groups, and other Learning Center services. It is significant to me because many new and international students hesitate to ask for academic help. Seeing the support explained on the page makes these services feel more approachable and shows that using them is a normal part of university life.

Requirement 2 of 3 · Embedded audio clip

Listen for a quick starting point

This audio clip summarizes how to use Resources, Events, Community, and Marketplace. It matters to me because students process information in different ways. A short spoken guide gives visitors an alternative to reading every section and communicates the welcoming purpose of the site.

Read the complete audio transcript

Welcome to Rutgers Student Link. I created this site to help new and international students find a clear starting point at Rutgers New Brunswick. Begin with the Resources page for official academic, health, transportation, and university services. Visit Events to discover campus activities, Community to connect with other students, and Marketplace to find useful secondhand items. My goal is to make the first weeks at Rutgers feel less confusing and more connected.

Requirement 3 of 3 · Three-image rotating carousel

Three highlights from the Rutgers experience

The carousel rotates through three different images automatically, and visitors can also use the arrow or dot controls. I chose Old Queens, Voorhees Mall, and Livingston Campus because they highlight Rutgers history, everyday student community, and modern learning spaces. Together they show that Rutgers is not one single building but a connected university with different environments and experiences.

Image sources: Old Queens via Wikimedia Commons; Voorhees Mall via Rutgers University; Livingston Campus via Rutgers University.
