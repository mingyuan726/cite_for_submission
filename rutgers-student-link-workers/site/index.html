Why this site exists

A clearer first step into campus life.

New students often receive important information across many different university pages, emails, and campus systems. This site gathers the starting points that are easiest to miss and explains why each one matters.

It is designed to help students move from “Where do I begin?” to a useful next action: find official support, register for an event, connect with a peer, or locate an affordable campus essential.

About the creator

Experience shaped around helping people adapt.

Hello, my name is Mingyuan Xue, and you can also call me Johnny. I am a student at Rutgers University-New Brunswick with experience in youth psychological counseling, content development, and teaching international students.

Through internships at the Dalian Youth Psychological Counseling Service Center and the Youth Mental Health Animation Production Base, I developed strong communication and organizational skills. I currently work with the Rutgers Global Teaching Department, helping international freshmen adapt to university life and preparing teaching materials.

I created Rutgers Student Link to turn that experience into a practical, student-centered starting point. I enjoy working with people, learning from different perspectives, and using my skills to support others.
